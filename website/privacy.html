The short version

Clip Engine records and stores your clips on your own PC. It does not upload clips, show ads or track what you do.

What stays on your PC

  • Your recordings, clips and the moments detected in them.
  • Your settings and the app's log file, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Clip_Engine.
  • Screen text read for detection. It is processed in memory by Windows and never leaves your PC.

What is sent over the internet

Only if you enter a license key: the key, and your PC's name as a label for the activation, are sent to Polar (polar.sh) to check that the license is valid. This happens when you activate and about twice a day after that.

When you buy

Purchases are processed by Polar Software Inc., our merchant of record. Polar collects your email address and payment details to complete the sale and send your license key. We receive your email address and order details from Polar so we can support you. See Polar's privacy policy.
