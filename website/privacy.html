The short version

Clip Engine records and stores your clips on your own PC. It doesn't show ads or track what you do. Clips go online only when you use a squad or the cloud library, and only the ones those send.

What stays on your PC

  • Your recordings, clips and the moments detected in them.
  • Your settings and the app's log file, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Clip_Engine.
  • Screen text read for detection. It is processed in memory by Windows and never leaves your PC.

What is sent over the internet

Nothing about you or your clips, unless you join a squad, sign in to the cloud library or add a Pro or Ultra license key. Clip Engine doesn't need an account or an internet connection to record.

The one exception is a short note the first time Clip Engine starts on a PC, so a friend who invited you gets credit for it (see Inviting friends).

To show a game's picture that isn't already on your PC, Clip Engine asks the Steam store for it once, by the game's name, and keeps it. Turn off Game pictures from Steam in Settings to use only the pictures on your PC.

Squads

When you're in a squad, Clip Engine's online service gets:

  • The name you pick, and an id for your PC.
  • Which game you're recording, and when each match started.
  • The moments you choose to share, with their time and the killfeed text.
  • Your views of squad moments. Which ones are uploaded follows your choice of highlights, everything or nothing.

Only your squad's members see these. Unstarred views are removed after [30] days. When you leave a squad, your uploaded views are deleted.

Cloud library

When you're signed in, the service keeps the name and handle you pick and the clips you upload, with their titles and moments. Unlisted clips play for anyone with the link. Public clips are also listed on your profile and in the hub. Private clips are kept as a backup, and their links play for no one. When you remove a clip, its file is deleted.

The service runs on Cloudflare. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

The hub

The hub lists the clips people set to Public, with their name, profile name and badge. Watching it needs no account and sets no cookies. If you sign in, the hub keeps a sign-in cookie in your browser for 30 days, or until you sign out, and nothing else. If you set a hub password, only a salted hash of it is stored. If you hide your Pro, Ultra or role badge, it's hidden everywhere others see your name. When you report a clip, the service keeps the clip's link, the reason and what you wrote, and not who sent it.

Friends

Friends are kept with your cloud account. When you add someone, they see your name and profile name. While Clip Engine is open, your friends see that you're online and which game you're recording, and, if Let friends join my squad is on, your squad's invite code. This is forgotten a few minutes after you close the app.

Inviting friends

The first time Clip Engine starts on a PC, it tells the online service so, with an id for the PC (a one-way hash of the id Windows gives it, which can't be turned back into it) and whether Clip Engine was new there. The service also sees the internet address the note comes from. It keeps the address only as a salted hash, used to check that an invited friend isn't on the same network as the person who invited them.

If you get an invite link, the service keeps the same kind of hashed address when you open it, for a week, so the app you install from it knows who invited you. If you share your own link, the service keeps your PC's hashed id, the hashed addresses you've used, the name you pick for the invite page, and how many friends have joined. It never knows who your friends are beyond that their PC counted.

Pro and Ultra

Subscriptions are sold by Polar, our merchant of record, which handles your payment details and tax and emails your license key. See Polar's privacy policy. Clip Engine sends your key to its online service, which checks it with Polar about twice a day. If you put the key on a cloud account, the service keeps it there to give the account its storage. If you choose a name for the credits, it's shown in the app and on this website for as long as your subscription runs; turn off Name in the credits in Settings to remove it.

Your email address, payment details and receipts stay with Polar and are kept private. Clip Engine never sees your card and doesn't keep your email. When the online service checks your key, it also asks Polar when your subscription next renews (or ends, if you cancelled), and passes only that date on to the app for a reminder a few days before; it keeps nothing about it. Use your own email at checkout: it's the only place your license key is sent.

Notifications

The app's notifications (friend requests, clips saved, your subscription renewing and the rest) and their history are kept only on your PC, in notifications.json next to the app's settings. Clear them from the bell in the sidebar.
