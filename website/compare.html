02In short

Where each one leads

Where Clip Engine leads

  • Your squad's views of every kill and revive, saved and lined up without anyone pressing a key.
  • Automatic clips in games with no events API, read from the HUD on your PC, and Detection Studio to add any other game.
  • Every audio source on its own track, up to 30 minutes of replay, and a share link that marks each moment.
  • No ads, no Overwolf, and nothing stamped on your videos, on any GPU.

Where others lead

  • Clip Engine's free cloud uploads stop at 1080p, 100 MB and 40 seconds; 4K, bigger and longer clips in the cloud need Pro or Ultra.
  • The NVIDIA App and AMD Software can record in AV1, and the NVIDIA App goes to 8K.
  • Medal and Outplayed clip some games Clip Engine doesn't yet, such as GTA V and War Thunder on Medal.
  • Xbox Game Bar and Steam need nothing extra installed if you already use them.
