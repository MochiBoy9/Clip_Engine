Free, Pro and Ultra

Clip Engine is free to use, recording up to 1080p with a 2 GB cloud library. Pro ($2.99 a month) records up to 1440p with 100 GB of cloud storage, and Ultra ($4.99 a month) records up to 4K with 250 GB, each with the extras listed on the website. Prices are in US dollars, before any tax Polar adds where you live.

Subscriptions are sold and renewed monthly by Polar, our merchant of record, under Polar's terms. Cancel any time from the link in your receipt; your plan runs to the end of the month you paid for. When a plan ends, recording drops to Free's resolution and the cloud library keeps your clips but takes no new uploads past Free's space until you remove some.
