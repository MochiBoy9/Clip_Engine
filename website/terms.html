Free, Pro and Ultra

Clip Engine is free to use, recording up to 4K, with a 2 GB cloud library that takes clips up to 1080p, 100 MB and 40 seconds. Pro ($2.99 a month) takes 4K clips of up to 1 GB and 10 minutes with 100 GB of cloud storage, and Ultra ($4.99 a month) 4K clips of up to 4 GB and 30 minutes with 250 GB, each with the extras listed on the website. Prices are in US dollars, before any tax Polar adds where you live.

Subscriptions are sold and renewed monthly by Polar, our merchant of record, under Polar's terms. Cancel any time from the link in your receipt; your plan runs to the end of the month you paid for. When a plan ends, recording carries on as before and the cloud library keeps your clips, but new uploads follow Free's limits and none go past Free's space until you remove some.

A license key is personal: use it on your own PCs and one cloud account, and please don't share it.
